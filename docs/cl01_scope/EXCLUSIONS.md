## PURPOSE

ISO/IEC 42001 allows exclusions when a requirement does not apply.
All exclusions must be justified. Unjustified exclusions invalidate conformance.

---

## EXCLUSION REGISTER

| ISO 42001 Clause | Requirement | Excluded | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.7.2 | Data management policy | PARTIAL | Retention/backup policy still open — see RISK_REGISTER.md R-D01 |
| A.8.2 | Supplier policy for AI | PARTIAL | Gemini (Google) is the only AI supplier, no formal supplier agreement process yet needed at this scale |

| A.10.2 | Third-party reporting | YES | No third parties process AVP_ERP data besides Google (Gemini API) |

---

## EXCLUSION RULES (ISO 42001:2023)

An exclusion is ONLY permitted when:
1. The requirement does not apply to the organization's activities
2. The exclusion does not affect the ability to ensure responsible AI
3. The exclusion is documented and justified here

**CANNOT be excluded:**
- Clause 4 (Context)
- Clause 5 (Leadership)
- Clause 6 (Planning)
- Clause 9 (Performance evaluation)
- Clause 10 (Improvement)
